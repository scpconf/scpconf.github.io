Conference Venue

The conference will take place at:

"Black Box", Maison des Sciences Humaines

11, Porte des Sciences, L-4366 Esch-sur-Alzette, Luxembourg

The conference will be hosted at the University of Luxembourg's Belval campus, in the "Black Box" event space of the Maison des Sciences Humaines. Belval is a vibrant, modern campus easily reachable by tram and train from Luxembourg City and Luxembourg Airport.

Open in Google Maps

Venue & Travel

By plane: Luxembourg Airport (LUX) is the nearest international airport. Tram T1 connects the airport directly to Luxembourg City centre and then to Belval-Université station (total ≈ 45 min).

By train / tram: Public transportation is free in Luxembourg (trains, trams, and buses) — no ticket needed. Take the train from Luxembourg City station to Belval-Université. Belval-Université station is within walking distance of the venue. Direct connections from Luxembourg City take approximately 20 minutes. Trains run frequently throughout the day.

By car: The Belval campus is accessible from motorway A4 (exit Esch-sur-Alzette). Paid parking is available on the campus.
